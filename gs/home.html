My story so far

I chose Industrial Engineering because I like problems where many moving parts have to fit together: people, stock, money and time. My early internships at Malia Group and MSD put me inside pharmaceutical production lines, where I saw that most delays came from coordination and planning, not from machines. That pushed me toward operations research.

In my last two years I have used those methods on very different problems. They include a mixed-integer model of electoral alliances, a battery dispatch optimization for a solar project at Mindsets, and the demand-forecasting system at the center of my Final Year Project. One lesson runs through all of them: a model is only useful if it is honest about its assumptions and if the people who use it can trust it.

Next, I plan to pursue graduate studies in operations research and AI-driven decision-making. I want to keep working where rigorous models meet real organizations.
